4. What the AI does, and what it doesn't

Gemini models check photos: whether a seal is intact, which code it shows, which items are visible, and how badly an item is damaged. These checks run on your device when it supports Gemini Nano, and otherwise in the cloud. They can be wrong.

AI never sets a refund amount. Fixed, published rules compute every amount and every liability split from the checked evidence. Claims whose evidence doesn't pass verification are never refunded automatically; they go to human review.
