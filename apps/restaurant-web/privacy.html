FairClaim is a hackathon prototype. This policy describes exactly what the demo does with data; it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

1. What we collect

  • An anonymous user ID created by Firebase Authentication. No account, name or email.
  • Demo order data: menu items, prices, order status, the 4-digit seal code.
  • The results of each AI check as structured data (for example: seal intact, code read, items visible, damage severity), with time stamps.
  • For a claim: the small crop of the photo around the item the customer tapped. Not the full photo.
  • For a dispatch: a SHA-256 fingerprint of the photo and, optionally, a small low-resolution thumbnail for the restaurant's own view.
  • For counter-evidence on a held claim: the restaurant's photo, which the customer of that order can also see.
  • App Check attestation that requests come from the genuine FairClaim apps.

We don't collect names, phone numbers, addresses, location or contacts. Every prompt tells the model to ignore people in the photo, and there is no face recognition.

2. Where photos are processed

On your device when it can run Gemini Nano (Chrome on a capable desktop, or a supported Android phone). The photo is checked locally and doesn't leave the device for that check.

In the cloud otherwise. On the cloud fallback the photo for that check is sent to the Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic. FairClaim doesn't store the full photo, but it does leave your device for that one check. Claim data (structured results only, no images) is also sent to Gemini for the consistency review and the written explanations.

If the demo runs on the Gemini API's free tier, Google's Gemini API Additional Terms allow Google to use content sent to it, including that photo, to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. On a billed project Google states it doesn't use prompts or responses to improve its products. Don't capture anything you wouldn't want reviewed.

Everything FairClaim stores lives in Firebase (Google Cloud).

3. Third parties

  • Google: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Remote Config, App Check, AI Logic), the Gemini API, reCAPTCHA Enterprise and Play Integrity.
  • Vercel: hosts this website's files and keeps standard request logs.

4. How long we keep it

Damage crops and counter-evidence photos are deleted automatically after 3 days. Claim and order data (structured results, no images) is kept until the demo is retired.

5. Your choices

The Android app has a "Delete my data" button that deletes your claims, crops and user record. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data and stopping use. Clearing this site's data in your browser gives you a new anonymous ID.

6. Notice under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

This page and the acceptance screen in each app are FairClaim's notice and request for consent. The personal data processed is listed in section 1; it is processed only to check evidence and decide simulated refunds. You can withdraw consent (section 5) and contact the grievance contact below about access, correction or erasure.

7. Security

Requests must carry an App Check token from the genuine apps. Firestore and Storage security rules limit each party to its own orders and claims, make evidence and decisions write-once, and cap amounts. All traffic is encrypted in transit.

8. Children

FairClaim is not for anyone under 18.
